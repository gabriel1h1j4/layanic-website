The Founder

Gabriel Indra Pandian

Founder & Solo Developer — Layanic Studio, Madurai, Tamil Nadu

Self-taught developer since the age of fourteen , Gabriel builds every layer of Layanic's worlds himself — engine systems, AI pipelines, and narrative design alike a discipline whose precision shows up directly in the motion and performance work behind Layanic's characters.

He holds an AI/ML certification BY IIT Patna, and was nominated for the Next Gen Kalam Medal for Individual Excellence (Translational Innovation) at The Next Gene Awards 2026, for the Ouroboros–Atom Theory — a configuration-based framework exploring bioelectric signaling in biological repair.
